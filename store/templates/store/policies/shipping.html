ATELIER STANDARDS

Shipping & Insured Transit Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Verified Pan-India Logistics

1. Dispatch Timelines

Every garment at Style Sphere Atelier undergoes rigorous tailoring inspection and is sealed in tamper-evident, dust-proof packaging. Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched within 24 business hours from our central logistics hub in Panchkula, Haryana.

2. Delivery Timeframes & Carriers

We partner exclusively with premium express air logistics networks (Delhivery Priority Air, Bluedart Express, and Xpressbees) to ensure safe, expedited delivery across all Indian pin codes:

  • Metro Destinations (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chandigarh): 2 to 3 business days.
  • Rest of India (Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities): 3 to 5 business days.
  • North-East & Special Regions: 5 to 7 business days.

3. Shipping Rates & Thresholds

• Complimentary Insured Shipping: Automatically applied to all orders with a cart subtotal of ₹999 or greater.
• Standard Transit Fee: A flat shipping charge of ₹99 is applied to orders below ₹999 to cover packaging and air insurance.

4. Real-Time Tracking (Air Waybill)

Upon parcel handover to the carrier, a unique Air Waybill (AWB) number (formatted as SS-EXP-XXXXXX) is generated and sent via automated confirmation email. Clients can monitor real-time checkpoint milestones directly within the Client Atelier Profile.

5. Cash on Delivery (COD) Protocols

Cash on Delivery is available across 99% of serviceable Indian postal codes. Please have the exact order amount ready in cash upon delivery. COD verification is confirmed via OTP or SMS link before dispatch.

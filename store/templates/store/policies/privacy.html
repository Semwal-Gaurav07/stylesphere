Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Encrypted Architecture

1. Commitment to Privacy

Style Sphere Atelier Inc. operates with strict confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties or marketing networks under any circumstances.

2. Information Collected

We only collect data strictly necessary to fulfill your streetwear orders and communicate order status:

  • Contact Details (Full name, email address, phone number).
  • Shipping Destination (Street address, city, state, and PIN code).
  • Account Credentials (Protected via PBKDF2 with SHA-256 cryptographic one-way hashing).

3. Payment Security & PCI-DSS Compliance

We do not store your complete credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers. All financial transactions are encrypted through 256-bit SSL encryption and processed directly by RBI-authorized payment aggregators (Razorpay).

4. User Rights & Account Erasure

You retain complete ownership of your data. You can view, update, or permanently delete your account history at any time by contacting privacy@stylesphere.in.
